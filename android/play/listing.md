## Full description (4000 max)
Dogear is an e-reader for people who read to think.

Open a PDF, EPUB, Word file, Markdown or plain text. It just reads. PDFs are reflowed into real text, so you can change the font size, the typeface and the line spacing the same way you would in an ebook. No pinching, no squinting.

When a passage lands, select it and tap one of five reactions:

💡 Aha. ❤️ Love. 🤔 Hmm. 😮 Whoa. 😤 Nope.

Add a note if you want. Bookmark the spot. That is the whole system, and it stays small on purpose, so your marks stay organised instead of becoming another pile.

Everything you keep lives in one place, the Marks tab, searchable and filterable by reaction and by book. Tap any mark and Dogear takes you back to that exact spot.

Connect Google Drive and your marks are written as tidy Markdown files into a Dogear folder in your own Drive: one file per book, one file per reaction, and an index. Read them on any device, feed them to your notes app, or just keep them.

Also included
• Table of contents, in-book search, reading progress and time left
• Four reading themes: Light, Sepia, Mist and Night
• Serif, sans and dyslexia-friendly typefaces
• Original page layout for scanned PDFs
• Share a file from any app straight into Dogear
• Works offline after the first open

Privacy
Dogear has no server and no account. Your books never leave your device. Drive sync is optional and uses only the permission to edit files Dogear created itself. No analytics, no ads.
