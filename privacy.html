Dogear privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Dogear is an e-reader. It runs entirely on your device. There is no Dogear server, no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

The books you add, your reading position, your reactions, notes and bookmarks are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. Dogear never uploads your books anywhere. Removing a book, or clearing the app's site data, deletes it.

Google Drive, if you connect it

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you connect it, Dogear writes your reactions, notes and bookmarks as Markdown files into a Dogear folder in your own Google Drive, so you can read them anywhere. Dogear asks only for the drive.file permission, which lets it see and edit files it created itself. It cannot read the rest of your Drive. Your books are not uploaded, only the text passages you reacted to and the notes you wrote.

The sign-in token Google gives Dogear is kept in the browser session only and is discarded when you close the app or tap Disconnect. Nobody other than you and Google handles this data. Dogear's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Dogear does not do

  • No data is sent to the developer.
  • No tracking, analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.

Network use

The app loads its own code, fonts and icons from the web the first time it runs, then keeps them cached for offline use. Those requests contain no personal data.
